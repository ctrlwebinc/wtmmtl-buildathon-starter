# 🌸 Code of Conduct — Women Techmakers (WTM) Montreal

All attendees, speakers, mentors, partners, volunteers, and organizers at the WTM Montreal Hackathon are required to agree with the following code of conduct. Organizers will enforce this code throughout the event.

The official text is available online at:  
👉 **[https://wtmmontreal.com/code-of-conduct/](https://wtmmontreal.com/code-of-conduct/)**  
*(Pour la version française, consultez [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md))*

---

## 🤝 Our Commitment

Women Techmakers Montreal is dedicated to providing a harassment-free and inclusive event experience for everyone, regardless of:
- Gender or gender identity and expression
- Sexual orientation
- Disability or neurodiversity
- Physical appearance or body size
- Age
- Race, ethnicity, or nationality
- Religion (or lack thereof)
- Technical background or level of experience

We do not tolerate harassment of participants in any form.

---

## 🚫 Zero Tolerance for Harassment

Harassment includes, but is not limited to:
- Offensive verbal comments related to gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, or religion.
- Sexual language and imagery in public spaces (including presentations, generated code, chat discussions, or slide decks).
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following.
- Harassing photography or audio/video recording.
- Sustained disruption of talks or workshops.
- Inappropriate physical contact and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Advocating for, or encouraging, any of the above behavior.

---

## 💻 Respect in Hackathon Code and Projects

As participants, we expect this spirit of respect and inclusion to be reflected in your hackathon projects:
- Do not build applications, tools, or prototypes that encourage hate, discrimination, harassment, or exclusion.
- Ensure that test data, sample texts, and user interfaces remain respectful and inclusive.

---

## 📢 Reporting & Immediate Intervention

If you are being harassed, notice that someone else is being harassed, or have any other concerns:

1. **Please contact an organizer or volunteer immediately** (identifiable by their badges, lanyards, or special t-shirts).
2. The organizing team is trained to intervene promptly and discreetly to help you feel safe (providing escorts, contacting venue security, or local law enforcement if required).
3. You can also reach out to the organizing team through the official event communication channels (Slack / Discord / WTM Montreal team email).

---

## ⚖️ Consequences

Participants asked to stop any harassing behavior are expected to comply immediately.

If a participant violates these rules, the WTM Montreal organizers may take any action they deem appropriate, from a formal warning to immediate expulsion from the hackathon without reimbursement, and a ban from future community events.

---

*Thank you for helping make the WTM Montreal Hackathon a welcoming, safe, and inspiring space for everyone! 💡✨*
